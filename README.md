# Custom Metal Plaque Design Guide

### By Dallas Metal Works

Welcome to the Custom Metal Plaque Design Guide, a resource dedicated to personalized metal signage, traditional craftsmanship, and thoughtful product design.

## About This Project

This repository shares useful information about designing and selecting custom brass and bronze plaques for residential, commercial, and commemorative applications.

## Key Topics

- Custom brass and bronze plaques
- Personalized house number and address signs
- Door name plaques and business signage
- Memorial and commemorative plaques
- Metal finishes, lettering, and decorative borders
- Plaque dimensions and mounting considerations

## Our Approach

At Dallas Metal Works, we believe every plaque has a purpose. Whether displaying a family name, identifying a property, or preserving a special memory, thoughtful design can make a lasting impression.
